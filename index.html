How to play

American checkers

The object

Occupy the opposing side of the board or remove every opposing piece. If a player has no legal move, the game is lost.

Moving

Pieces rest on dark squares and move one square diagonally forward. A white piece moves up the board; a black piece moves down. Kings move one square diagonally in either direction.

Capturing

Jump over an adjacent enemy piece into the empty square beyond. Captures are mandatory. If several captures are available, any one may be chosen.

Multi-jumps

If a capture can continue, the same piece remains selected and you must choose another valid jump. You cannot switch pieces mid-sequence.

King promotion

When a piece reaches the far row, it becomes a king and receives a raised metallic red crown. A newly crowned king may continue a capture sequence.

Draws

A game is drawn after forty moves by each side without a capture or a man move, or when the same position occurs three times.
